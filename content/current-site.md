## About / Our story (from /about-5)
**Heading:** The story behind the vision

Yanicke Lim Sang hails from the island of Jamaica and has made Hawaii her home for the past 17 years, and counting. Jamaica is known for its cultural diversity, which explains her background of Chinese, African, and East Indian heritage. Growing up in a multicultural family, she developed a deep appreciation for diversity. Her keen eye for detail and vibrant colour palettes fuelled her passion for fashion and textile design. She earned her degree in Fashion Illustration and Design from Miami International Art University and dedicated several years to the fashion industry before shifting her focus to motherhood.

She gained valuable experience working as an apprentice for event planners over a period of five years. She also managed concert coordination for emerging talent at a record label in Miami Beach. With extensive expertise in event coordination and design, she founded Zenju Weddings and Events of Hawaii, LLC in 2010. The business, named after her two sons Zen and Jude, remains closely managed by Yanicke, reflecting her commitment and dedication to the brand.

**Our vision** (source is all caps):
Our dedicated team of creative and diligent designers are committed to executing your design vision seamlessly within the overall event layout on the day of your occasion. Our experienced team will meticulously manage every detail, transforming your vision into reality with care and dedication. We pride ourselves on creating unique, elegant, and memorable celebrations that leave a lasting impression on you and your guests. ## Packages (from /packages)
Intro: "Our packages can be customized to suit your budget and style." Prices for all packages are discussed during the consultation.

### Ku'u Lei: Full Wedding Coordination
"Ku'u Lei" means "Beloved" in Hawaiian. Exclusive full coordination package covering every detail from the day the date is set. Unlimited emails, phone calls, and consulting.
Includes: 2-hour consultation · questionnaire · storyboard design · unlimited emails/calls/video meetings · unlimited face-to-face meetings (2 weeks' notice) · quotes, budget plan and analysis · vendor negotiation · preferred vendor list · vendor contracts and payments · timeline · emcee script · 30-day checklist · rehearsal · full Zenju staff on the day · assistant in the hotel room · layout · set up · oversee all vendors on the day · registration · ushering guests · décor clean-up and gift table pack-up · site check, last ones to leave.

### Ho'opono: Day-of Coordination
"Ho'opono" means faithful in Hawaiian. For couples who want day-of coordination only.
Includes: rehearsal · Zenju staff of 2 · timeline management · design consultation · set up and break down · coordination of the ceremony and reception · unlimited emails · timeline · emcee script · layout · receiving packages. No planning outside the day of the event. Customizable.

### Barefoot Nuptials: Elopement
For couples who want a secluded, no-fuss ceremony.
Includes: coordination · oceanfront venue for 3 hours (ceremony only) · bride's hair and makeup · haku (tropical floral crown) · bridal bouquet · groom's lei · 1 hour photography · 1 hour videography · 1 hour live stream · officiant (kahu) · 1 hour ukulele musician · chairs · bamboo arch. Ceremony only, no reception (no furniture or structures allowed on state beaches). Customizable.

### Events (non-wedding): Full Coordination
Birthdays, retirements, anniversaries, bachelor/bachelorette parties, concerts, corporate events.
Includes: questionnaire · finding a venue · storyboard design · budget · contract negotiation · layout · checklists · rehearsal · walk-through · decorating · full Zenju staff · timeline · emcee script · vendor coordination · set up · registration · overseeing the emcee · clean up. Custom pricing after the initial consultation.
